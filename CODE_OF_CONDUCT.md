# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Help people learn. Don't be the reason someone leaves security work.

## Our standards

Things that make this community good:

- Patience with people who are learning. Everyone was a beginner once.
- Giving credit, sharing knowledge, and explaining your reasoning.
- Accepting feedback and critique on your work gracefully.
- Keeping the focus on authorized, ethical security practice.

Things that aren't welcome:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Encouraging or facilitating illegal or unauthorized activity.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Gatekeeping or belittling people for what they don't yet know.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and in public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the contact listed on the repository profile. Reports will be handled discreetly. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban participants who violate this code.
